description=Migrate CAD, KML/KMZ and Shapefile data to PostGIS with automated validation and reports
about=Imports DXF, KML, KMZ, Shapefile, GeoJSON and GPKG data, maps it to a target schema with reusable JSON templates, reprojects and cleans it, and checks it with ten configurable rules (duplicates, missing values, invalid domains, dangling cable ends, out-of-extent features and more). Clean features are loaded to PostGIS in a single transaction; features with problems are quarantined in a validation_errors layer and table. Produces a PDF and CSV report. Works from a dialog and as a Processing algorithm. Rule sets and templates are plain JSON; example telecom templates are included.
